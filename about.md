    <h2>What CRX Is</h2>
    <p>CRX is an iOS app for tracking medications, including schedules, reminders, refills, and symptoms, and for keeping a record you can bring to your doctor or pharmacist. It's built for people managing real regimens: older adults and anyone living with a chronic condition.</p>

    <h2>Our Mission</h2>
    <p>CRX was built with a simple belief: <strong>medication management should be calm, clear, and private.</strong></p>
    <p>Managing medications is already hard, and an app shouldn't add to it. CRX aims to make sense for real people: reliability over novelty, clarity over clutter.</p>

    <h2>Design Principles</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Calm over urgency:</strong> No alarming colors or anxious UI. Medication management is already stressful enough.</li>
      <li><strong>Accessibility first:</strong> Not an afterthought — a core requirement from the first line of code.</li>
      <li><strong>Respect for your data:</strong> No ads, no cross-app tracking, no selling data. You can delete your account and data in the app at any time.</li>
      <li><strong>Clarity always:</strong> Every label, every action, every screen should be unambiguous.</li>
      <li><strong>Honest about limits:</strong> CRX is a tool, not a doctor. It says so, plainly, wherever it matters.</li>
    </ul>
