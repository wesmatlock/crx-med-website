<section class="feature-group">
  <h2>Adding medications</h2>
  <dl class="facts">
    <div><dt>Prescription and barcode scanning</dt><dd>Scan a prescription label or a package barcode and CRX fills in the name, strength, and form for you to review.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Drug database search</dt><dd>Search RxNorm, from the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Vitamins and supplements are matched against the NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Pill details</dt><dd>See the color, shape, and imprint of each pill from FDA label data, and add up to six photos per medication.</dd></div>
  </dl>
</section>

<section class="feature-group">
  <h2>Staying on schedule</h2>
  <dl class="facts">
    <div><dt>Doses and reminders</dt><dd>Daily, weekly, interval, and as-needed schedules, with reminders at the times you choose. Log a dose with one tap.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Today view</dt><dd>The next dose with a countdown, later doses grouped by time of day, and your as-needed medications.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Widgets and Live Activity</dt><dd>Home Screen widgets show your next dose and today's schedule, and a Lock Screen Live Activity lets you log a dose without opening the app.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Siri shortcuts</dt><dd>Log doses and symptoms with Siri.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Refills and pharmacies</dt><dd>Days'-supply tracking estimates when you'll run out and reminds you a week before. Save pharmacies once and call them with a tap. Expiry dates are tracked too.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Works offline</dt><dd>Your medications, schedules, and history live on your device. Changes sync across your devices when you're back online. Medication search and cloud AI features need a connection.</dd></div>
  </dl>
</section>

<section class="feature-group">
  <h2>Checking for interactions</h2>
  <dl class="facts">
    <div><dt>AI drug-safety checks</dt><dd>Optional AI analysis reviews your list for potential drug-drug interactions, conflicts with your recorded allergies, and food interactions, in plain language. Checks use a cloud AI model. Before the first check, CRX shows what it sends and lets you accept or turn the checks off.</dd></div>
  </dl>
  <p class="notice">AI checks may not catch every interaction, and a missing warning does not mean a combination is safe. Always check with your doctor or pharmacist.</p>
</section>

<section class="feature-group">
  <h2>Tracking and reporting</h2>
  <dl class="facts">
    <div><dt>Symptoms and Insights</dt><dd>Log symptoms alongside your doses, then see adherence percentages and streaks over 7, 30, or 90 days, with your doses and symptoms charted side by side.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Apple Health</dt><dd>Optionally write dose records to Apple Health, and let CRX read vitals like blood pressure, glucose, weight, and heart rate for Insights.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Doctor visit export</dt><dd>Generate a PDF with your medications, dose history, refills, symptoms, and the drug safety analysis, plus CSV dose-history export.</dd></div>
  </dl>
</section>

<section class="feature-group">
  <h2>Accessibility and language</h2>
  <dl class="facts">
    <div><dt>VoiceOver</dt><dd>Controls and medication details are labeled for VoiceOver.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Dynamic Type</dt><dd>Supports iOS text sizes, including the accessibility sizes.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Reduced Motion</dt><dd>Respects the iOS Reduce Motion setting.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Tap targets</dt><dd>Built to Apple's 44-point minimum.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Not color alone</dt><dd>Status is designed to use icons and text, not just color.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Seven languages</dt><dd>The interface is in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, and Portuguese (Brazil). Drug databases are U.S. sources, label scanning reads English text, and AI explanations are in English.</dd></div>
  </dl>
</section>
