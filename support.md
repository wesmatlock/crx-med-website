    <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

    <h3>Is CRX available now?</h3>
    <p>CRX is coming soon to the App Store. Check back here, or email us to be notified when it launches.</p>

    <h3>My reminders aren't showing up. What should I check?</h3>
    <p>First, make sure notifications are allowed: open iOS <strong>Settings → Notifications → CRX</strong> and confirm Allow Notifications is on. Also check that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing alerts, and that the medication has a schedule set in the app. Notification delivery is ultimately controlled by iOS and is not guaranteed, so please don't rely on CRX as your only reminder for critical medications.</p>

    <h3>How do I delete my account and data?</h3>
    <p>In the app, go to <strong>Profile → Account → Delete Account</strong>. This removes your account and your data from our servers and clears the data stored on your device.</p>

    <h3>Can I export my data?</h3>
    <p>Yes. CRX can build a PDF report for your doctor or pharmacist with your medications, dose history, refills, symptoms, and the drug safety analysis. You can also export your dose history as a CSV file.</p>

    <h3>Is my health data private?</h3>
    <p>We use your data to run the app for you. We don't sell it, show ads, or track you across other apps. Some features send information to outside services, such as cloud AI for safety checks. See our <a href="/privacy/">Privacy Policy</a> for exactly what is sent and the choices you have.</p>

    <h3>Does CRX work offline?</h3>
    <p>Yes. Your medications, schedules, and history are stored on your device, so you can view and log doses without a connection. Changes sync automatically when you're back online. Some features, like medication search and cloud AI safety checks, need a connection.</p>

    <h3>What do I need to run CRX?</h3>
    <p>CRX runs on iPhone and iPad and requires iOS 26 or later. On-device AI features also require Apple Intelligence to be available and enabled.</p>

    <h3>What languages does CRX support?</h3>
    <p>The interface is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, and Portuguese (Brazil). The drug databases are U.S. sources, so search works best for products sold in the United States. Label scanning reads English text, and AI explanations are written in English.</p>

    <h3>Does CRX support VoiceOver?</h3>
    <p>Yes. CRX is built with accessibility as a core requirement, including VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type, and large touch targets.</p>
